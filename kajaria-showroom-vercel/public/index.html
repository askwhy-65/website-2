Good choices start with good questions

Looks matter. So does how your space lives.

The right tile is more than a colour or pattern. Think about foot traffic, water exposure, maintenance, lighting and the feeling you want the room to create.

  • ✓ Choose by use first.
    Consider whether the surface is for a wet area, a busy floor or a decorative wall.
  • ✓ Compare finish and scale.
    Tile size, grout lines and light can change how a room feels.
  • ✓ Confirm the details.
    Check exact size, shade, batch, slip rating and current availability before ordering.

Questions before you decide

A few helpful things to know.

Every project is different, so treat these as starting points and confirm exact product specifications before buying.

Which tile finish works best for a bathroom?

Choose based on the exact application and water exposure. Ask for the manufacturer's slip-resistance information and use a suitable floor tile rather than assuming any matte tile is anti-slip.

How much extra tile should I order?

A 5–10% allowance is a common starting point for straightforward layouts, but diagonal patterns, complex cuts, breakage and future replacement needs can change it. Confirm the allowance with your installer.

Can I check price and availability online?

This website is designed to help you explore styles and send an enquiry. Current price, shade batch, stock, delivery and product specifications should be confirmed directly with the showroom.

What should I bring when visiting the showroom?

Bring room measurements, photos, a cabinet or paint sample if available, your approximate budget and any contractor recommendations for tile size or technical performance.
