Privacy policy

How enquiry details are handled on this website.

This template policy should be reviewed and updated by the business owner before launch. It is not legal advice.

Information you submit

If you use the enquiry form, the website collects the name, phone number, optional email address, topic of interest and message you submit. Enquiries are stored on the website server so the business can respond.

How information is used

Submitted details are used to respond to your enquiry, coordinate a showroom conversation and maintain an enquiry record. If email delivery is configured, the enquiry may also be emailed to the configured business inbox.

Retention and access

Enquiry records are stored in the website’s data directory on the hosting server. Access to the admin dashboard should be restricted to authorised staff. The owner should establish an appropriate retention and deletion process.

Third-party services

If configured, email delivery and WhatsApp links are handled by their respective service providers. Their privacy terms may apply when you use those services.
