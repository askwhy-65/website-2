Choosing tiles can feel overwhelming. Our aim is to make the process more considered: start with your space, compare the right details and build a shortlist that suits your needs.

Our approach

Not just a pattern. A decision you’ll live with.

The tile that looks beautiful in a photo may behave differently in a wet bathroom, a sunny balcony or a high-traffic entrance. We encourage customers to look beyond appearance and ask the practical questions too.

  • ✓ Understand the room.
    Start with use, traffic, moisture, cleaning habits and who will use the space.
  • ✓ Compare samples in context.
    View colour and texture under realistic lighting, alongside your other materials.
  • ✓ Confirm before ordering.
    Check manufacturer details, shade and batch consistency, quantity, delivery and return terms.

The details matter

What we encourage every customer to check.

01

Technical fit

Confirm slip performance, water absorption, recommended application and installation requirements with the manufacturer.

02

Colour & batch

Check the product code, shade variation, calibre and batch before ordering, especially across multiple rooms.

03

Project planning

Verify quantities, delivery access, breakage allowance, installer needs and the terms that apply to your order.
